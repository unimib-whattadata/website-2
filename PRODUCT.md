## Platform

## Users

Whattadata speaks to organisations that need to turn research, data and artificial intelligence into practical decisions.

For Arianne, patients and therapists have equal importance. Patients need continuity, reflection and secure access around therapy; therapists need an integrated way to deliver sessions, monitor progress and manage clinical work.

For WhattaDocs, the primary audiences are organisations working with large internal document collections and professionals in regulated or evidence-sensitive settings who need to verify answers against source material.

For WhattaFlow, participants who contribute wearable data and the research teams who organise, monitor and analyse a study have equal importance. Participants need a low-friction Garmin connection and clear continuity; researchers need structured cohorts, visibility into data flow and exportable data.

## Product Purpose

The Whattadata website explains the company, its services and its products at a high level, then routes visitors to the right next step.

Arianne connects the moments between therapy sessions with the clinical encounter. Its Whattadata product page should help patients and therapists understand the shared ecosystem, then continue to the dedicated Arianne website.

WhattaDocs turns long-document question answering into an inspectable workflow. Its Whattadata product page should explain how answers, citations and source context stay connected, then invite interested organisations to contact Whattadata at info@whattadata.it.

WhattaFlow connects participant onboarding to the operational side of wearable-data studies. Its Whattadata product page should show the continuous path from Garmin connection to project and group organisation, data-flow monitoring, analysis and export, then route visitors to the participant portal while keeping the researcher portal available.

## Positioning

Arianne is a mental-health ecosystem built around both sides of care: patients can reflect and monitor between sessions, while therapists can run and follow the therapeutic path through connected digital tools.

WhattaDocs is evidence-centric rather than answer-only: users ask within a defined document scope, inspect the passages behind an answer, compare them in context, judge their support and refine the inquiry from what they verified.

WhattaFlow is a two-sided wearable-study workflow rather than a consumer fitness product: participants connect and synchronise their Garmin data, while research teams organise studies and keep the resulting data flow visible and usable.

## Operating Context

Arianne supports online and in-person therapy journeys. The product context includes patient and therapist dashboards, video sessions, diaries and monitoring, questionnaires and psychometric scales, progress review, appointments and secure sharing.

WhattaDocs supports work with heterogeneous, long-form documents. The product context includes document upload and indexing, projects, active document scopes, conversational queries, evidence references, source inspection, notes and continued inquiry. The supplied study describes both self-hosted and managed configurations.

WhattaFlow supports research and clinical-study operations around wearable data. Its documented context includes Garmin participant onboarding, invited researcher access, projects and research groups, device and synchronisation monitoring, alerts and notifications, data availability and compliance views, analytics, configurable raw-data export and standard or clinical reports.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The product pages are English-language, high-level overviews within the main Whattadata website; they do not replace either product's dedicated commercial or operational experience.
- Every product page uses the same high-level information set: value proposition and route, one authentic application screenshot, audiences, four-step workflow, four core capabilities and one final action.
- Arianne must give equal narrative and visual weight to patients and therapists.
- Arianne's final primary action leads to https://arianne.whattadata.it/.
- WhattaDocs must address both organisations with internal knowledge bases and professionals in regulated settings.
- WhattaDocs' final primary action opens an email to info@whattadata.it.
- WhattaFlow must give participant and researcher paths equal narrative and visual weight while showing that they belong to one continuous system.
- WhattaFlow's final primary action leads to https://whattaflow-partecipants.whattadata.it/; the researcher portal at https://whattaflow.whattadata.it/ remains a secondary route.
- WhattaFlow may describe research operations and data continuity, but must not imply that it is a consumer fitness tracker or make clinical-outcome claims.
- Do not invent pricing, customers, testimonials, certifications, deployment guarantees or performance claims.
- Research metrics may be shown only with their study context and must not be generalized into commercial guarantees.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the established Whattadata identity and the documented “The Living Data Lab” visual system.
- Preserve the Whattadata, Arianne, WhattaDocs and WhattaFlow names. Use the supplied logo assets, including the official indigo-and-lime WhattaFlow mark exported from the brand design system; do not reconstruct product marks with live text.
- Keep the site voice direct, evidence-aware and approachable in English.
- Preserve the existing indigo-and-lime cube language; product-specific imagery can extend it without replacing it.

## Product Principles

1. Explain the mechanism before making the claim.
2. Let patients and therapists see themselves with equal clarity in Arianne.
3. Keep evidence inspectable and claims bounded in WhattaDocs.
4. Keep participant contribution and researcher operations visibly connected in WhattaFlow.
5. Give every page one explicit next step.
6. Use technical precision in service of comprehension, not as a costume.
7. Keep the three product pages structurally equivalent and remove research provenance or repeated feature lists from the visitor-facing overview.
